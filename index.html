Multi-Sided LiteUpper Platform

LiteUpper is a multi-sided education technology platform that I designed and developed to support different types of users and operational requirements through interconnected systems and APIs.

1. User / Student Side

The regular user side provides students with access to educational and examination features, including practice questions, learning materials, mathematics videos, CBT examinations, instant results and performance tracking.

  • Student accounts and authentication
  • Examination and CBT participation
  • Practice questions
  • Learning videos and educational content
  • Instant results and feedback
  • Academic performance tracking

2. Institution / Teacher Side

The institution and teacher side is designed for schools, teachers and educational institutions to manage their academic activities and monitor student performance.

  • School and institution management
  • Teacher accounts and permissions
  • Student management
  • Question and examination management
  • CBT creation and administration
  • Results and academic analytics
  • Class, term and session performance tracking

3. Company / Admin Backend

LiteUpper also has an internal backend and administrative system used for company operations and management of the platform's ecosystem.

  • Platform administration
  • User and institution management
  • Content management
  • Question and examination administration
  • Platform analytics
  • Subscription and account management
  • Operational and company activities
  • Backend monitoring and maintenance

These different interfaces communicate with the central Django / Django REST Framework backend through APIs, with PostgreSQL managing application data. The architecture allows students, institutions, teachers and LiteUpper administrators to interact with the same platform while having different roles, permissions and workflows.

Offline CBT System

One of the major systems I developed for LiteUpper is an offline Computer-Based Testing platform designed for schools that may not have reliable internet connectivity.

The CBT system runs through a local server, allowing computers connected to the local network to access examinations without requiring continuous internet access.

The system supports examination management, student participation, automatic scoring, result processing, violation monitoring, theory answers and synchronization of examination data.

This architecture allows schools to conduct CBT examinations even when internet connectivity is unavailable.
